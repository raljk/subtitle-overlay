Subtitle Overlay settings

Keys are stored only in this browser profile and are sent only to the provider they belong to. Nothing else leaves your machine: subtitle files are parsed locally, and in live mode only short audio segments are sent for recognition.

Speech recognition (live subtitles)
Needed for live subtitles: the tab audio is sent to Whisper a few seconds at a time (about $0.36 per hour of video with whisper-1). Get a key.
whisper-1 works with both live pipelines. The gpt-4o transcribe models only work in “transcribe + translate”.
Point this at a local OpenAI-compatible server (for example http://localhost:8000/v1 running faster-whisper-server) to keep the audio on your machine; the key is then optional.
Translation (subtitle files, and the bilingual live pipeline)
Claude translates subtitle files and live transcripts. Get a key.
claude-opus-5-5: best quality ($4 / $20 per million input / output tokens). claude-sonnet-5-5: $2 / $10. claude-haiku-5-5: fastest and cheapest ($0.10 / $0.50), a good pick for live mode. A full-length film is a few tens of thousands of tokens, so even Opus costs well under a dollar per film.
Used only when translating with OpenAI.
